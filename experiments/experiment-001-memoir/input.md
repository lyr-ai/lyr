# Source passages — "The Long Way Home" (memoir, excerpts)

The raw passages the semantic records in `semantic_nodes.json` were drawn from.

**chapter-01.** When I stepped off the plane in Toronto in the winter of 1990, I
was twenty-six and I did not know that I would never again live in the same
country as my mother. We had said goodbye in Kraków as if it were ordinary.

**chapter-02.** Every morning began the same way: a cup of black coffee, no sugar,
carried to the desk before the light came up.

**chapter-03.** They offered me the regional directorship in 1998. I said no. My
parents were getting older and I could not imagine being a plane ride away.

**chapter-04.** In those years — my thirties — I kept everything in cash. I had
watched savings vanish once and I would not risk it again.

**chapter-05.** In 2005 the company wanted to send us to Singapore. It would have
been good for my career. But the children were settled in their school, and I
would not uproot them.

**chapter-06.** I wrote at that same desk for thirty years, the coffee going cold
beside me.

**chapter-07.** The startup was the most exciting work of my life, and in 2012 I
walked away from it without hesitating, because Marek was sick and there was no
one else to sit with him.

**chapter-08.** I wrote to my sister every week. Thirty years of letters, never a
week skipped, long after everyone said no one writes letters anymore.

**chapter-09.** People ask if I regret the promotion I turned down in 1998. I
never have. Not once.

**chapter-11.** And then, at sixty-three, I did the thing my younger self would
have called insane: I put a third of my retirement into my nephew's restaurant,
which had never served a single meal.
