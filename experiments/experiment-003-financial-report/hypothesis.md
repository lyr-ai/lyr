# Experiment 003 — Financial report: hypothesis & what to watch

**Question:** same unchanged builder, on structured financial disclosures — does it
separate durable facts from routine line items, and handle a guidance reversal?

| Records | Case | Hoped-for behavior |
|---|---|---|
| 0, 1 | duplicate description of one event (the acquisition, in 10-Q and on the call) | grouped as **one** evidence; a durable **fact/decision** — significant one-off |
| 2 | a sustained policy (12th consecutive dividend raise) | plausibly durable as a **pattern/policy**, even from one line — the "12th consecutive" carries the recurrence |
| 3, 4 | guidance **reversal** (raised in Q1, lowered in Q3) | a **contradiction across periods** — scope by quarter or take the later value; do **not** silently average or drop |
| 5 | a single quarter's growth metric | likely **not** durable on its own (one-quarter number) — watch |
| 6, 7 | identical routine rent expense, two quarters | **NO_OP** — recurs but trivial; and the two are *not* independent support for anything |
| 8 | one-time impairment | a notable one-off event; durable fact or NO_OP? watch the reasoning |

**Watch for:** does "routine" / "one-time" language in the records steer it
correctly? Does it treat the two identical rent lines as accumulating evidence
(it shouldn't)? Does the guidance reversal get scoped by period?
