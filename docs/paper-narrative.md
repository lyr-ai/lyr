# Paper Narrative — locked before drafting

*Not a review of the theory. A review of the narrative. The rule: if the three questions
below cannot be answered crisply, do not start writing. They can. This file is the scaffold
the paper is built on — the boundary of what is in and what is out.*

---

## Q1 — Delete all the math. One sentence: what does the paper say?

> **World-level identification is not a property of an explanation; it is the output of
> executing an auditable procedure that progressively externalizes hidden assumptions.**

If this sentence is not the spine of every section, the section is off-center.

## Q2 — Delete all the theory. Keep only the experiments. What remains?

Three things — and they must be enough to carry the paper alone:

- **Two worked examples.** A (diary — world/measurement compensation); B (agent log —
  censoring, no likelihood, formalization split).
- **Three structural falsifiers.** Auditor determinism → forced `F`; `F`-stability → folds
  into `Θ*`; unbounded `M` → audited, not solved.
- **Three rounds of independent reproduction.** Nine executions by strangers; invention list
  driven to empty.

If the experiments did not reduce to exactly these, they were not designed around the central
claim. They were. Everything else is derivation.

## The named contribution

The paper's new object is **an auditable identification protocol.** Its defining property —
the thing no prior framing captured — is:

> **The protocol continuously externalizes hidden assumptions.**

Four witnesses that this is what the recent work actually demonstrated (none of them argues the
theory is *smarter*; all four argue it *surfaces what it does not know*):

1. **Example A corrected its own authors' intuition** — "did she change?" was expected to be
   identified; the protocol showed it was not (compensation). An assumption the authors held
   was externalized as a required measurement constraint.
2. **`F` was not a patch — a falsifier produced it.** Natural-language ambiguity was located,
   with a witness, in the formalization step; `F` entered as a multi-valued, audited class,
   not an author's convenience.
3. **Unbounded `M` was audited, not solved.** The framework does not delete `m*`; it makes
   bounding `M` a *tagged* act, distinguishing an assumption from an empirical-under-commitments
   restriction with the *same* identified set.
4. **Phase 1 converted six tacit premises into explicit ones** — `A₀`, the `F`-split, `A_cause`,
   `A₀ᴮ`, sharpened `A_schema`, the `h_X`-cell clarification — each surfaced by a stranger
   getting stuck, not by the authors introspecting.

## The deeper discipline (one level below the claim)

> **Every new object must arrive with a witness.**

This constrains the *research process*, not just the theory. `F` arrived with a witness
(falsifier 1). The `M`-restriction arrived with a witness (Stage 3 calibration). Each premise
arrived with a witness (an executor could not continue without it). The protocol is the first
thing in LYR that disciplines its own authors — which is why it, and not a new layer, is the
mature result.
