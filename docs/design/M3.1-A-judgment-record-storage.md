# M3.1-A Decision Note — JudgmentRecord Storage

**Scope:** M3.1-A only. This note records the chosen hypothesis for how a
`JudgmentRecord` is stored and linked. It **does not resolve Q1/Q2 in code** and
introduces **no schema migration** — it fixes the design decision that the
M3.1-A implementation step will later follow.

Parent design: [`M3.1-llm-guided-durable-consolidation.md`](./M3.1-llm-guided-durable-consolidation.md)
(Open Questions Q1, Q2).

---

## Decisions

### D1 — Storage model: immutable append-only record in the primary store

A `JudgmentRecord` is stored as an **immutable, append-only record in the primary
store**, alongside `SourceRecord`s and `Node`s — **not** modeled as a `Node` and
**not** placed in a separate execution-log system.

- It is not a `Node`: no `layer`, no `identity`/`version` chain, no participation
  in provenance walks or the active-memory view. It is an *audit artifact*, not a
  unit of knowledge.
- It lives in the primary store so a judgment and the durable version it produced
  are queryable together, with one persistence and one backup boundary.
- Append-only: a record is written once and never mutated. A later judgment that
  supersedes an earlier one **appends a new record**; it never edits the old one.

*Resolves Q1 (design level):* primary store, not a sidecar log — but with
append-only semantics, so it keeps the log's auditability without a second system.

### D2 — Evidence grouping: stored explicitly inside the judgment, no global identity

Evidence grouping is stored **explicitly inside each `JudgmentRecord`** (the
model's "these describe one observation / these are independent" judgment), for
audit and evaluation.

- Groups are **local to the judgment** that made them. They do **not** get global
  identities, are **not** deduplicated across judgments, and are **not** first-class
  knowledge nodes.
- This gives persistence and reproducibility of the grouping decision without
  standing up another entity system prematurely.

*Resolves Q2 (design level):* explicit-and-embedded, not derived and not globally
identified. Promoting groups to first-class entities remains deferred (see
[Still deferred](#still-deferred)).

### D3 — Minimum linkage: judgment ↔ durable version

The minimum required to connect a judgment to the durable version it produced,
in **both** directions:

**On the `JudgmentRecord` → durable version** (already implied by
`final_engine_action` in the parent doc):

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `id` | the judgment's own identifier |
| `created_at` | ordering / audit |
| `input_semantic_ids` | what evidence the judgment considered |
| `final_engine_action.operation` | `ADD` / `UPDATE` / `MERGE` / `NO_OP` (or reject) |
| `final_engine_action.identity` | the durable identity affected |
| `final_engine_action.version` | the exact durable version produced (null for NO_OP/reject) |
| `final_engine_action.node_id` | the produced `Node`'s id (null for NO_OP/reject) |
| `final_engine_action.rejection_reason` | why nothing was committed, if applicable |

**On the durable `Node` version → judgment** (the only new, engine-owned link):

- a single `judgment_id` recorded in the node's engine-owned `attributes`,
  naming the `JudgmentRecord` that produced **this version**.

That one back-pointer is the whole linkage. It makes judgment provenance a direct
lookup (`durable version → its judgment`) and knowledge provenance unchanged
(`durable version → semantic evidence`). No join table, no group identities.

> A `NO_OP` or a rejected proposal still produces a `JudgmentRecord` (so the
> attempt is auditable) but produces **no** durable version and therefore **no**
> `judgment_id` back-pointer — the linkage is one-directional in that case.

### D4 — Retention and replay

- **Retention:** every judgment attempt is retained permanently — accepted,
  `NO_OP`, and rejected/failed alike. Volume management (archival/compaction) is
  out of scope for M3.1-A and explicitly deferred.
- **Two distinct replay meanings, kept separate:**
  - **Engine replay (deterministic, in scope):** re-applying the recorded
    `final_engine_action`s in order reconstructs the durable state exactly, with no
    model calls. This is the replay M3.1-A guarantees — it is what makes the durable
    layer reproducible and auditable from the record alone.
  - **Model replay (non-deterministic, not guaranteed):** re-running the recorded
    inputs through the recorded model+prompt version may differ. The record stores
    enough (`provider` / `model` / `prompt_version` / params / `run_id`) to
    *attempt* it and to diagnose drift, but bit-exact reproduction is not promised.

### D5 — What remains immutable

- **`JudgmentRecord`:** immutable once written. Corrections are new records.
- **Durable `Node` versions:** immutable and content-addressed (unchanged from M3).
- **The `judgment_id` back-pointer** on a committed version: immutable — it names
  the judgment that produced *that* version; a later change is a new version with
  its own `judgment_id`.
- **`SourceRecord`s:** immutable (unchanged from M1).

Mutable-by-append only: the *set* of judgments and durable versions grows; nothing
already written is edited in place.

---

## Consequences

- No new entity system: one immutable record type + one attribute back-pointer.
- Judgment provenance and knowledge provenance are both O(1) lookups from a durable
  version.
- Auditing "why does this durable belief exist, and what did the model reject on the
  way?" is answerable entirely from retained records.
- Reproducibility of the *substrate* (engine replay) is guaranteed; reproducibility
  of the *model* is best-effort and explicitly labeled as such.

## Non-goals for M3.1-A

- No code, no dataclass, no store method, no migration in this note.
- No global/first-class evidence-group entity.
- No retention/compaction policy.
- No guarantee of bit-exact model replay.

## Still deferred

- Whether evidence groups are ever promoted to first-class, globally-identified
  entities (revisit only if cross-judgment group reuse proves necessary).
- Storage-volume management for the append-only judgment set.
- Exact serialization/schema of `JudgmentRecord` (decided when M3.1-A is
  implemented, following D1–D5).
