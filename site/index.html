The difference

Traditional AI memory

  • Stores documents or embeddings
  • Returns the closest text
  • Can't say why it believes something
  • Forgets how understanding changed

LYR

  • Forms durable knowledge from evolving evidence
  • Explains why a memory exists — proposal, verdict, commit
  • Verifies whether something deserves to persist
  • Traces every belief back to its original source

Four layers over immutable evidence

Source→ Semantic→ Durable→ Cognitive · next

Every layer above Source cites its evidence one layer down — so any belief is a single walk back to what actually happened. The Durable layer is model-driven and was evaluated on a frozen four-domain benchmark (M3.1). The Cognitive layer is the next layer — future work, not yet built.
