Data — ethically obtained, or not at all

Provenance integrity starts at acquisition. A case whose evidence begins "obtained without consent" has a corrupted first witness.

No Facebook Group API. No unauthorized scraping. Every observation records source, timestamp, channel, evidence type. LYR never reports certainty the evidence doesn't support.

The goal is not prediction. The first public case will answer "what diagnosis is currently identified?" — with competing hypotheses, supporting evidence, measurement assumptions, remaining uncertainty, and the evidence that would most reduce it.
