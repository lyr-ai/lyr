About

Identification, made auditable.

A record — a log, a diary, a forum thread, a model's explanation of itself — is the world seen through an unknown measurement. An explanation that fits the record is not thereby true of the world. LYR is a protocol that says exactly what a record does and does not identify, and under which assumptions.

Three lines, mutually non-interfering

① Theory
Frozen. What is auditable identification? Reopens only on a real-world contradiction the protocol cannot express.
② Method
The paper. How does someone else execute the protocol? Open to clarity / positioning / reproducibility.
③ Applications
Continuous. Is the protocol useful in the real world? Accumulates real cases. Only these can reopen the theory.
Status: research complete, awaiting external evidence. This is not "finished" — it is a statement about where the uncertainty now lives: no longer in whether the protocol is sound, but in whether people outside its making can use it.

The discipline

  • Don't add an object without a witness.
  • Don't claim a limitation without a witness.
  • Use real evidence, never generated examples — and ethically-obtained over merely obtainable.
  • The protocol is fixed; a real case it can't express reopens the research, it doesn't get patched.
  • The number that matters is not cases completed — it's how many forced the theory to reopen.
